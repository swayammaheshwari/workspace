# Malai Paneer

A rich, creamy paneer curry made with cashew-based gravy.

## Ingredients

- 250g paneer, cubed
- 2 tbsp ghee or oil
- 1 large onion, roughly chopped
- 2 tomatoes, roughly chopped
- 10-12 cashews
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1-2 green chilies
- 1/2 cup fresh cream (malai)
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander leaves, for garnish
- Water as needed

## Instructions

1. **Prep the paneer**: If using firm paneer, lightly pan-fry the cubes in a little ghee until golden on the edges. Set aside. Soak cashews in warm water for 15 minutes.

2. **Make the base paste**: In a pan, heat 1 tbsp ghee and sauté the onions until translucent. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies, cook for 1-2 minutes until the raw smell goes away. Add tomatoes and cook until soft. Let the mixture cool, then blend with the soaked cashews into a smooth paste.

3. **Cook the gravy**: Heat the remaining ghee in the same pan. Add the blended paste and cook on medium heat for 5-7 minutes, stirring often, until it thickens and the oil starts to separate.

4. **Season**: Add turmeric, coriander powder, salt, and a splash of water to adjust consistency. Simmer for 3-4 minutes.

5. **Add cream and paneer**: Lower the heat, stir in the fresh cream, and mix well. Add the paneer cubes and gently simmer for 3-5 minutes so the paneer soaks up the flavor. Avoid boiling vigorously to keep the cream from splitting.

6. **Finish**: Sprinkle garam masala, mix gently, and turn off the heat.

7. **Garnish and serve**: Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and a drizzle of cream. Serve hot with naan, roti, or jeera rice.

## Tips

- For extra richness, add a pinch of sugar to balance the tomato tang.
- Soaking paneer cubes in warm water for 10 minutes before adding keeps them soft.
- Cashew paste can be substituted with melon seeds or almonds for a similar creamy texture.
