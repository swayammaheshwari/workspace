# Dal Tadka

A comforting lentil curry finished with a smoky, spiced ghee tempering.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup toor dal (split pigeon peas)
- 1/4 cup masoor dal (split red lentils)
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- Salt to taste
- 3 cups water, for cooking the dal
- 2 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
- 2-3 dried red chilies
- 3-4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1/2 tsp red chili powder
- 1/4 tsp asafoetida (hing)
- Fresh coriander leaves, for garnish

## Instructions

1. **Cook the dal**: Rinse the toor dal and masoor dal together until the water runs clear. Pressure cook with turmeric, salt, and water for 4-5 whistles, or simmer in a pot for 25-30 minutes until soft and mushy. Mash lightly and set aside.

2. **Start the tempering**: Heat ghee in a small pan over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and mustard seeds, letting them sputter. Add the dried red chilies and asafoetida.

3. **Build the flavor**: Add sliced garlic and sauté until golden. Add the chopped onion and cook until translucent, then stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.

4. **Add tomatoes**: Add chopped tomatoes and red chili powder, cooking until the tomatoes soften and the oil begins to separate.

5. **Combine**: Pour the tempering into the cooked dal and stir well. Add water to adjust consistency and simmer for 5-8 minutes so the flavors meld.

6. **Garnish and serve**: Top with fresh coriander leaves and a final spoon of ghee tempering if desired. Serve hot with steamed rice or roti.

## Tips

- Mixing toor and masoor dal gives a good balance of body and creaminess.
- For extra smokiness, try the dhungar method: place a small piece of hot charcoal in a bowl over the dal, add a drop of ghee, and cover for 2 minutes to infuse smoke.
- Adjust water at the end depending on whether you want a thicker or soupier dal.
